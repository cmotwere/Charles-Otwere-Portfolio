1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ about.name|default:"Charles Otwere" }} ("I", "me", or "this website") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit this personal portfolio website. This site is operated for the purpose of showcasing professional work, projects, and enabling visitors to get in touch — it does not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

By using this website, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please refrain from submitting personal information through the site.

2 Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you use the contact form, I collect the details you submit: your name, email address, subject, and message content. This is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage & analytics data — pages visited, time on site, referring pages, browser and device type, and approximate location (via Google Analytics).
  • IP address — used briefly for spam and abuse prevention (e.g. rate-limiting contact form submissions).
  • Cookies & local storage — used to remember your display theme (light/dark) and to power analytics, as described in Section 4.

3 How We Use Information

  • To respond to messages sent through the contact form.
  • To understand how visitors use the site and improve its content and performance.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to spam, abuse, or malicious activity.
  • To remember your site preferences, such as theme selection.

I do not use your information for advertising, and I do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

4 Cookies

6 Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for a reasonable period afterward for reference, after which they may be deleted. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' default retention settings.

7 Data Security

This site is served over HTTPS and uses standard safeguards — including CSRF protection, rate limiting, and spam filtering on the contact form — to protect information in transit and reduce misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but reasonable measures are taken to protect your information.

8 Your Rights

If you are located in the EU or UK, data protection law (GDPR) gives you rights over your personal information regardless of this policy. As a matter of good practice, I extend the same rights to all visitors, regardless of location, including the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information held about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including analytics tracking.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

This site is operated by an individual, not a business subject to the CCPA, but California residents' requests will be honored on the same voluntary basis. To exercise any of these rights, contact me using the details in Section 11.

9 Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and personal information is not knowingly collected from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please contact me so it can be removed.

10 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practices or for legal and operational reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
